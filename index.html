Which Laptop Should You Buy?

Not sure what specs you need? Here is our simple recommendation for your setup.

🎓

For College Students

You need a laptop that is fast, lightweight, and can handle typing essays, opening dozens of research tabs, and running video calls without slowing down.

  • Recommended RAM: 8GB to 16GB for smooth multitasking.
  • Storage: 256GB+ SSD for instant startup and file saves.
  • Best Choice: MacBook Pro or Dell Latitude. Apple MacBooks offer incredible battery life and build quality, while Windows laptops like the Dell Latitude give you ultimate software flexibility.
👓

For Seniors & Everyday Use

You want a laptop that is simple, reliable, easy to read, and hassle-free for browsing the web, checking emails, watching videos, and staying in touch with family.

  • Screen Quality: Crisp Retina or Full HD display for clear reading.
  • Ease of Use: Simple operating system with fast, reliable responsiveness.
  • Best Choice: MacBook Pro / Air or a clean Windows 11 PC. MacBooks offer simple, intuitive navigation, while Windows laptops provide familiar traditional controls at a budget-friendly price.
